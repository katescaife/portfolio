A learning game by Kate Scaife

Signal & Noise

Your job: you're the solutions architect on a new security deployment. From a catalog of 48 detections, enable the set that best protects this specific customer, before the clock runs out.

01Every detection has a hidden point value based on fit for this customer, strong fits score high, poor fits score negative.
02Every detection also generates an estimated alert load. The customer's SOC can absorb 35 alerts/day. Exceed it and your score bleeds. Watch the meter.
03Your final score is multiplied by your precision, good picks ÷ total picks. Shotgunning dilutes itself.
04Results are graded against the best achievable portfolio, so your percentage means something.

More detections ≠ better security. Curate.
