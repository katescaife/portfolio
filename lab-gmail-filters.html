A filters lab, built to be broken

Original practice module · built for this portfolio

This short, simple practice lab walks through setting up an email filter on a free, public product. Many similarly built learning experiences over-guide the user, or even prevent them from clicking anything but the right object. I've always felt that limits how well the knowledge sticks. Instead, I build experiences where the user has little guidance and can make the wrong choice, then see the result or get feedback. Curious why this is a from-scratch demo instead of a work sample? Why this exists

Most of the case studies on this site are proof from inside a company: things I built for a real team with real constraints. This one is different on purpose. When I couldn't show screenshots of client or employer work without permission I didn't have, I built something new instead of describing my approach in the abstract. It's a small, simplified example, but it's real.
